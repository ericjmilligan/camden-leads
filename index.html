How it works

Every Saturday we pull owner records from each county's GIS and tax records from each county collector, then flag the properties worth a closer look.

Recent foreclosure

Bank-owned

Title held by a bank, mortgage servicer or credit union, acquired in the last three years. Usually a foreclosure (REO).

HUD / VA

Government-owned

Homes held by HUD, the VA, Fannie Mae or Freddie Mac after a foreclosure. Usually sold through agency listing sites.

2+ yrs unpaid

Tax-delinquent

Two or more years of property taxes unpaid. Paying them doesn't make it yours: buy from the owner, or bid at the August tax sale.

Unsold at tax sale

Fourth-sale list

Offered at three tax sales with no buyer. Missouri residents can request these at the county collector's office.
