Camden County Property Leads

Bank-owned properties and parcels with two or more years of unpaid property taxes in Camden County, Missouri.

Sources: Camden County GIS parcel records and the Camden County Collector's tax portal (public records), updated weekly. Bank-owned means the deedholder is a bank, lender, HUD or the VA; "long held" bank parcels are often branch buildings. Fourth sale parcels were offered at three tax sales without a buyer and can be requested at the Collector's office. Total due includes interest, fees and the current year's bill.

Information may be out of date or incomplete. Verify everything with the Camden County Collector (573-346-4440) and Recorder of Deeds before acting. Nothing here is legal or financial advice.
